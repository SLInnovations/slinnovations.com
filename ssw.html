Structured Steps to Writing

Writing instruction taught the way you teach reading.

Explicit, systematic writing instruction for 1:1 and small group. 15 minutes a lesson. Everything scripted.

Browse the shop Not sure where to start? Take the screener

"What do you use for writing?"

It's the question we hear more than any other. And for a long time, we didn't have a good answer either.

Reading has a roadmap. Sounds, syllables, morphology, each one taught explicitly, one step at a time. Writing often gets a prompt and a blank page. So we spent years piecing things together for our own students.

Structured Steps to Writing is the roadmap we wished we'd had. Every skill is taught directly, practiced right away, and built on in the next lesson. It all starts with one sentence.
