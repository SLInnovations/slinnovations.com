Who these suit

Tutors and resource teachers working 1:1 or in small groups, and homeschool families who want an explicit editing and brainstorming routine without committing to a full program.

The differentiated versions exist because students in the same group are rarely in the same place. A student who needs every step spelled out and a student ready to work independently can use the same tool at the same table.

These tools run through the whole program
