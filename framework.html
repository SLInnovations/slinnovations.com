The step between sentences and paragraphs.

A student finishes Foundations. They can write a complete sentence, expand it, vary it, and combine ideas. Then the task changes to "write a paragraph about sea otters," and the whole thing falls apart.

Five correct sentences sitting next to each other is not a paragraph. Most students have never been told what the difference is. They have been shown finished paragraphs and asked to produce one, which is not the same as being taught how.

Unit 5 teaches it the same way Foundations taught sentences. One skill at a time, explicitly, before the next one is introduced.

Every sentence has a job

STEMS gives students a job for each sentence in a paragraph, so they always know what the next sentence is supposed to do.

STEMS
S
State your topic. Tell the reader what the paragraph is about.
T
Target your important idea. Name the most important detail.
E
Explain with an example. Give one fact or example.
M
Make it matter. Tell why it matters.
S
Sum it up. Wrap up the main idea in fresh words.

Lesson 1 opens with SPARK+SORT for paragraphs, so students gather and sort their ideas before they write a word.
